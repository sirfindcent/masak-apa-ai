# Soto Ayam (Indonesian Chicken Noodle Soup)

**Description:** Soto Ayam is a traditional Indonesian chicken soup infused with aromatic spices like turmeric, lemongrass, and galangal. It's typically served with vermicelli noodles, shredded chicken, and various toppings like boiled eggs, celery, and fried shallots.

**Ingredients:**
- Whole chicken
- Water
- Vermicelli noodles or glass noodles
- Lemongrass
- Kaffir lime leaves
- Turmeric
- Ginger
- Garlic
- Shallots
- Hard-boiled eggs
- Bean sprouts
- Celery, chopped
- Fried shallots
